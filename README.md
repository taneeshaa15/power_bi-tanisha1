# 🏥 U.S. Healthcare Dynamics: An In-depth Analysis 

Welcome to the **U.S. Healthcare Dynamics Dashboard**, a comprehensive Power BI project focused on uncovering key insights from the American healthcare system.

---

## 📊 Overview

This interactive Power BI dashboard explores the evolving landscape of U.S. healthcare, offering a data-driven narrative across five core analytical perspectives:

- **Executive Summary**: High-level trends, KPIs, and financial highlights.
- **Hospital Analysis**: Performance metrics, patient outcomes, and operational insights across healthcare institutions.
- **Patient Analysis**: Demographics, lifestyle behaviors, and healthcare experience across 84,000+ patients in all 49 states.
- **Payer-Provider Dynamics**: Financial and operational relationships between insurers and healthcare providers.
- **Monthly Expenses Overview**: Expense trends with insights into optimization and efficiency.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Empower stakeholders with data-backed decision-making tools.
- Illuminate hidden inefficiencies and opportunities for cost control.
- Support providers, payers, and policymakers in enhancing care quality and access.
- Foster a shared understanding of healthcare dynamics to influence positive change.

---

## 🔍 Features

- Regional filtering for Midwest, Northeast, South, and West U.S.
- Deep-dive into gender, exercise, diet, tobacco/alcohol use, and blood type trends.
- CPT (Current Procedural Terminology) unit analysis by provider, specialty, and hospital.
- Real-time insights into monthly revenue, payment efficiency, and expense tracking.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI Desktop** for data visualization and reporting
- **Data Model** includes patient, provider, financial, and procedural datasets
